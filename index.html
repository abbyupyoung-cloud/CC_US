Q&A

Common questions from distributors, laundromat owners, OPL buyers, and service partners.

Q What territories are currently open for distribution?▾

Availability varies by region and product line. Share your target market on the contact form below and our team will confirm whether the territory is open, semi-exclusive, or already assigned.

Q What's the minimum order quantity for distributors?▾

Minimum order quantities depend on the product line and your agreed territory size. Our team will outline specific volume tiers and wholesale pricing during onboarding.

Q How do I become an Authorized Service Partner (ASP)?▾

Apply through the contact form and select "ASP Partner." Qualified applicants complete factory training and certification before being added to our authorized service network.

Q Do you provide installation and technical training?▾

Yes. Distributors and ASP partners receive factory training covering installation, routine maintenance, and troubleshooting for both Takurox OPL and Coin equipment.

Q What warranty coverage comes with Takurox equipment?▾

All Takurox equipment ships with a manufacturer warranty; exact terms vary by model and configuration. Extended coverage plans are available for OPL installations.

Q How is pricing structured for OPL vs. Coin equipment?▾

OPL pricing is typically quoted per project based on capacity and facility requirements, while Coin equipment follows standard unit pricing with volume discounts for multi-store orders.
